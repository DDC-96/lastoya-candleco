# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Candle buyers who want something with genuine meaning behind it — people drawn to craft, cultural roots, and intentional living. They're choosing against mass-market products on purpose. They browse slowly, read the label, care about what's in their home and why. Age ranges broadly but skews toward 25–45 adults who value provenance and story over discount codes. They find Lastoya because they were looking for something they hadn't found yet.

## Product Purpose

Lastoya Candle Co. is a small-batch, hand-poured candle brand based in Riverside, CA. The name comes from the Zapotec language of Oaxaca's valleys and mountains — "Lastoya" means "my heart" or "my love." That origin is not backstory; it's the brand's reason for existing. Every candle is poured to order in small batches, never rushed and never made for scale. The site's job is to make that meaning tangible: to sell candles in a way that feels as intentional as making them.

## Brand Personality

Bold · Cultural · Proud

Voice: Direct and unhurried. No filler, no hedging, no manufactured warmth. The brand knows what it is and says so without apology. Zapotec heritage is stated plainly, not performed. The tone carries weight without being heavy.

## Anti-references

- **Luxury minimalism (Aesop, Diptyque)**: Cold, austere, clinically spare. Puts the brand above the customer. Lastoya is intimate and close, not aspirationally distant. No matte serif on white, no near-nothing layouts that signal "we are too refined to explain ourselves."
- **Instagram wellness brands**: Pastel palettes, rounded fonts, bubble lettering, motivational quotes, girlboss energy. Surfaces-only aesthetics with no cultural substance. Nothing pink, nothing soft-launch, nothing "self-care" as a concept.
- **Generic Etsy-aesthetic**: Kraft paper textures, handwritten fonts, twine-and-mason-jar craft fair energy. Reduces meaningful slow craft to folksy kitsch. The Zapotec connection is specific and earned — it cannot live in a visual language that equates handmade with rustic.

## Design Principles

1. **Heritage is the product, not the backstory.** The Zapotec origin of the name is Lastoya's core differentiator — not a tagline added at the bottom of an About page. Every design decision should honor the weight of that origin.
2. **Slowness is confidence.** A brand that pours to order and never rushes should feel that way. Generous pacing, no element screaming for attention, no urgency mechanics.
3. **Bold without loudness.** Bold personality doesn't mean aggressive. It means standing clearly for what you are: no beige neutrality, no hedged palettes, no apologetic copy. The dark room aesthetic is the right register.
4. **Specificity over warmth.** "Small-batch, hand-poured in Riverside, CA" is better than "crafted with love." The brand's warmth comes from being specific and true, not from adding warmth words.
5. **The candle is the hero.** Photography and scent language carry more emotional weight than any decorative pattern. Design should step back and let the product and its story do the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components. Keyboard navigability for all interactive elements. Meaningful focus indicators. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — animations should degrade to instant transitions, not disappear.
